## Goal
Three bugs/gaps found on device after Wave 5 (lesson 2 = `modes_v1`, its `items.json` currently has 0 words/sentences/qa):
1. **Stale title:** open lesson 1, then lesson 2 → the player header still shows lesson 1's title "Đồ ăn mang về của đồng nghiệp". Cause (PM): `PlayerViewModel.ensureLessonMeta` (~line 280) returns early when `lessonId` already matches and `lessonTitle != null` — the title of the previous lesson survives a lesson switch. The title (and the other per-lesson meta: format, practice/zh availability) must belong to the lesson currently playing.
2. **Nghe "Hiện câu" shows only pinyin, no hanzi.** Source lines have hanzi (`lessons/lesson_002_nghe.json`, e.g. `我们听故事。`). Find why the revealed line renders without hanzi (suspect: hanzi rendering depends on word tokens from `items.json`, which is empty for lesson 2). A line must always show its hanzi, with or without item/word data, in every mode and lesson 1.
3. **Copy a sentence (user):** long-press a transcript line → copies **only its hanzi** to the clipboard + a short confirmation ("Đã sao chép"). Purpose: paste into a translator. All lessons and modes; in Nghe/Phản xạ only lines that are currently visible can be copied (no leaking hidden answers).
