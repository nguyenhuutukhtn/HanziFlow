# 03 — Core phrases and word tiers

Lesson 3: **午饭点错菜** (lunch delivery, wrong dish). Author: AI A (Qwen).
Reads: `01_brief.md`, `content/workflow.md` → "Rules for every step", "Line format",
"Independent lessons", "Vocabulary balance".

Conventions used in this file:

- Line format: `ref | speaker | 中文 | pinyin | Tiếng Việt`. One sentence per line.
  Speakers: `zhang_jing` (张静, female), `liu_yang` (刘洋, male), `coach_zh` (narrator, male).
- Pinyin is written **as pronounced** (sandhi for 一 / 不, neutral tones: `yào bu yào`,
  `dǎ ge diànhuà`, `kèqi`, `guòlai`). If the project prefers underlying tones, step 4 flags it.
- Vietnamese: colleagues of the same age → self `mình`, other `bạn`. 张静 = "Tĩnh",
  刘洋 = "Lưu Dương" when a name is used inside a Vietnamese gloss.
- Bands (guidance for steps 5 and 9, not gates): Core phrases 10–30 hearings each,
  Core words 10–40, Coach ≥ 8. No content word above ~60.
- Situation: two colleagues in the office at lunchtime. 刘洋 orders 外卖 on his phone for
  both of them. Two boxes arrive; 张静's box is a very spicy dish she did not order.
  All facts are fixed in `04b_facts.md` — the example sentences below already agree with it.

### 2.5 Function — free, no cap

Grammatical items, question words, courtesies and time/place words the story may use
without being counted as new content words:

Notes:

- 想 / 要 / 能 / 会 / 知道 / 给 are treated as Function here (auxiliary-like, extremely
  frequent). If step 4 prefers them inside Core words, moving them changes no sentence.
- 中午 / 现在 / 一会儿 are time words, treated as Function; 分钟 / 点(o'clock) are in Core
  words because the lesson drills numbers.
- 死 in 饿死了 (p6b) is a degree complement, not a content word → Function; 对 (p14b) and
  哥 (外卖小哥) likewise. 过来 以后 半 份 个 were used but sat in no tier (step 4, #10).
- 转 is a **Core word**, not Function: 我转你 (p14b, p15a, F14) is the natural way colleagues
  settle a lunch, and it replaces the longer 我给你钱.

## Open doubts

1. **Pinyin convention**: written as pronounced (`bú shì`, `yí fèn`, `kèqi`, `guòlai`).
   Confirm this matches lessons 1–2 / the pipeline; otherwise step 5 must rewrite all pinyin.
2. **吃 → ngật or cật?** and **面 → miến (麵, noodle) vs diện (面, surface)**: simplified
   Chinese merges them; step 10 (two independent Hán-Việt lists) must settle these, plus
   豆腐 → đậu hủ (Vietnamese everyday word is "đậu hũ/đậu phụ").
3. **红烧肉饭 vs 红烧肉盖饭**: the app dish name is usually 盖饭 (adds 盖). I chose the
   shorter 红烧肉饭 for character budget. Step 5 may switch if the PM allows +1 character.
4. **麻婆豆腐饭**: I made the wrong box a rice box (parallel to 红烧肉饭). If step 5 prefers
   plain 麻婆豆腐 (no rice), the facts still hold — but pick one and keep it in every mode.
5. **备注 "不要辣"**: kept as a Core word because it is very authentic (you write it in the
   app) and it proves 刘洋 ordered correctly. If step 4 finds it too niche, drop the note and
   the fact "he ordered right" rests on the 订单 alone (`04b_facts.md` F8).
6. **Paying** — resolved by the step-4 review (#1, #2): `p15a`/`p14b` use 我转你, 我给你钱 is
   retired (it reads as handing cash and contradicts the cashless F7). 转 is a Core word;
   `04b_facts.md` F14 records the form.
7. **外卖员 vs 骑手** — resolved by the step-4 review (#3): the tier has 外卖小哥 (colloquial,
   gloss 小哥); 骑手 is allowed as the app's own wording and is no longer banned. 外卖员 is
   retired (news/official register).
8. Exposure bands are guidance only (workflow): no count fixes requested at this step —
   the PM's script supplies character and length counts.
