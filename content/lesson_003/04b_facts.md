## Resolved here (doubts 3, 4, 6 — plus 5, which F8 depends on)

- **Doubt 3 → 红烧肉饭.** Shorter, fits the character budget; 盖 adds nothing to the plot.
- **Doubt 4 → 麻婆豆腐饭.** Parallel to 红烧肉饭 (two 饭 boxes from one 商家) and 饭 is already
  Core, so the only new reading is 麻婆豆腐. Every mode uses the full string; V4 is Nghe-only.
- **Doubt 6 → 我转你** (F14), per step-4 review findings 1–2. 我给你钱 reads as handing cash,
  which F7's cashless app payment rules out; 我转你 is what colleagues actually say and is
  shorter than 给…钱. 转 (zhuǎn) is now a Core word in `03_core.md`.
- **Doubt 5 → keep 备注 "不要辣"** (F8): what the app shows, and what proves 刘洋 ordered right.
