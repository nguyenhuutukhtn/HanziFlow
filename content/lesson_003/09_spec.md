# 09 spec — five modes for lesson 3 (午饭点错菜)

Input: `05_story.md` (story v2, approved — the "script"), `03_core.md` (tiers),
`04b_facts.md` (facts F1–F19, canonical answers, Nghe variants). One mode per run, one file
per mode: `09_hieu.md`, `09_tuvung.md`, `09_nghe.md`, `09_phanxa.md`, `09_noi.md`; the PM
joins them into `09_modes.md` by script. Each file: one ``` block of rows, then
`## Open doubts`.

## Rules for all modes

1. **Top priority: natural, common spoken Chinese.** No known-words restriction (lessons are
   independent); prefer words of the story and `03_core.md` tiers, add a new word only when the
   explanation needs it and it is simple and everyday. No grammar limits.
2. Every line agrees with `04b_facts.md` (Phản xạ answers use its canonical short answers;
   retellings use only its V variants).
3. Line format, one per row: `ref | speaker | 中文 | pinyin (tone sandhi as pronounced) | Tiếng Việt`.
   speaker: `narrator` (= the male coach_zh voice), `zhang_jing`, `liu_yang`, `coach_vi` (Hiểu only),
   `gap`. A gap row: `<ref>.g | gap | — | — | —` (learner answers or repeats there).
   Full-width Chinese punctuation (，。？！).
4. Vietnamese voice **only in Hiểu**. Other modes still fill the Tiếng Việt column (on-screen
   transcript) but have no `coach_vi` rows.
5. `coach_vi` rows contain **no Chinese characters** (the Vietnamese voice can't say them):
   the Chinese word is its own `narrator` row right before; the Vietnamese row says
   "từ này" / "từ vừa nghe" or uses Hán-Việt. Chinese column of a coach_vi row = `—`.
6. Each Chinese-only mode opens with one fixed short line naming the mode (works cold).
7. Instructions reuse a few short lines: 我们听故事。/ 你回答。/ 跟我说。/ 你说。/ 再听一遍。

## Hiểu (6–8 min) — refs h…
1. coach_vi intro, 2–3 sentences (what happens, what to notice: ordering, 送错了, paying back).
2. Story line by line: the story row (its speaker), then coach_vi with its meaning.
3. Gloss each core phrase P1–P15: narrator says the phrase, coach_vi short meaning (+ Hán-Việt
   when it helps), narrator repeats one story line using it.
4. Story once more, Chinese only.

## Từ vựng (6–10 min, Chinese only) — refs v…
Like lesson 2: per core word/phrase (~12–15 items, the most useful from P1–P15 and the Core
words): narrator says it, says it twice, explains it in **very simple Chinese** (what it is,
when you say it), one or two example lines (story lines keep their speaker), then
"<word>。你说。" + gap. Opener e.g. 今天我们听几个词。

## Nghe (6–10 min, Chinese only) — refs l…
1. Opener (我们听故事。). 2. Story once. 3. Third-person retelling by the narrator (no
dialogue). 4. 3–4 retellings changing ONE detail each, each opened by a short "this time"
line, short and logically consistent; announce that it is a different version. Use 04b's V
items for wording; changed-detail ideas: 张静 orders 红烧肉饭 too; the spicy box is 刘洋's;
the shop says 二十分钟; 张静 likes spicy food and eats it. 5. Story once more.

## Phản xạ (6–10 min, Chinese only) — refs q…
1. Opener (你回答。). 2. 40–50 questions over the facts: yes/no (吗, 是不是), choice (还是),
short wh- (谁, 什么, 几, 多少, 怎么办). Easy → hard, each building on the previous.
3. Per question: narrator question → gap → narrator short answer → narrator full answer.
Exactly one correct answer and a clear subject per question.

## Nói (5–8 min, Chinese only) — refs s…  (use `n` refs to avoid clashing with story refs: n1…)
1. Opener (跟我说。). 2. Build-up for the 8 most useful dialogue lines: back-chain from the
END (送错了 → 是商家送错了 …), 3–4 steps per line, each step a row + a gap row.
3. Shadowing: every zhang_jing / liu_yang story line, each + a gap row (no narrator lines).
