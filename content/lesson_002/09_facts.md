## World

- A座 and B座 face each other: **A座在B座对面** (and B座在A座对面). Neither is "beside" the other.
- The café is beside **B座** (咖啡店在B座旁边). **A座旁边没有咖啡店.**
- 门口 = the place in front of a door, where people go in and out (门前，进出的地方). It is outside; 大厅 is inside the building: 我从门口进去，里面是大厅.
- 大厅 is on 一楼. 楼下 is relative ("below", here: down at street level under 八楼); it is not a synonym of 一楼.
- 陈东's office floor: A座八楼. Meeting is in A座.
